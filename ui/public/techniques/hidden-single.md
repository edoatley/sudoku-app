# Hidden Single

A **Hidden Single** happens when a number can only be placed in exactly **one specific cell** within a row, column, or 3x3 block, even though that cell might have other candidates written in its notes.



## How it works
While a *Naked* Single looks at one cell and rules out 8 numbers, a *Hidden* Single looks at one Number (e.g., the number 5) and rules out 8 cells within a group.

If a 3x3 block has five empty squares, but four of those squares are "seen" by a 5 in an intersecting row or column, then the 5 is forced into the one remaining square. It was "hiding" among the other possible pencil marks!

## The Action
When you find a Hidden Single:
1. You can confidently write that number into the cell.
2. Erase all other pencil marks from that cell—they don't matter anymore!
