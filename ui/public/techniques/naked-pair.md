# Naked Pairs

A **Naked Pair** occurs when exactly two cells in the same group (a row, a column, or a 3x3 block) contain **exactly the same two candidates**—and no other candidates.



## How it works

Imagine two cells in the same row that both only have the candidates **3** and **7**.

We don't know *which* cell is the 3 and *which* cell is the 7 yet. However, we know with 100% certainty that one of them must be the 3, and the other must be the 7.

Because those two numbers are completely "locked up" by those two cells, **no other cell in that row can contain a 3 or a 7**.

## The Action

When you find a Naked Pair:
1. Identify the group they share (Row, Column, or Block).
2. Look at all the *other* empty cells in that exact same group.
3. **Erase** the two paired numbers from the pencil marks of those other cells.

*Note: Eliminating these candidates often immediately reveals a Naked Single or another easy move!*
