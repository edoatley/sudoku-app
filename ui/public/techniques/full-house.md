# Full House

The **Full House** is the easiest move to spot in Sudoku. It happens when a single row, column, or 3x3 block already has 8 out of its 9 cells filled in.



## How it works
Because every group (row, column, or block) must contain the numbers 1 through 9 exactly once, if you see a group with 8 numbers already placed, there is no logic or guessing required. The remaining empty cell *must* be the 1 missing number.

## How to spot it
1. Scan the board for rows, columns, or 3x3 blocks that look almost completely full.
2. Count the numbers 1 through 9 in that specific group.
3. Whichever number you didn't say out loud is the answer. Fill it in!
